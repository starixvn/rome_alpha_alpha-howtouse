1. Introduction

    Cricket betting has seen significant growth since the emergence of dedicated sports channels and bookmakers. Match Winner is the most basic cricket bet involves betting on the winner of a match.

    In our system, we configure Match Winner’s settings with the same settings as Sportsbook − Other Sports. Besides that, we also offer feature “Net Position” to Match Winner for enabling members to play more bets. We will explain more details about Net Position in the next section.

  2. Net Position
    1. Definition
      Net Position is the minimum payout that member could get from his tickets bet on Match Winner of each match.
      • If the payout is greater than the total actual stake, the Net Position is the total actual stake.

        Actual Stake = Stake deducted in the balance.

      • This amount is added into member’s Bet Credit to enable the member to play more bets.
      • After the match is settled, Net Position of the match will be cleared and the actual winning/losing amount will be added to member balance.
    2. Location
      Net Position amount is displayed in Balance Info at member level only.
    3. Examples
      • Example 1: Total Payout Home Wins > Total Stake > Total Payout Away Wins
        On bet type Match Winner, member A has placed stake $100 for Home team. The possible outcome of this ticket will be:
        Bet Tickets Possible Payout
        # Bet type Bet team Stake Odds Home Wins Away Wins
        1 Match Winner Home 100 1.94 194 0
        Total 100 194 0
        According to the table above:
        - If Home team wins => Payout Home Wins = $194 => Net Position = 0
        - If Away teams wins => Payout Away Wins = 0
        - Total Actual Stake = $100
        Member Balance Info will be displayed as below:
        Member Balance Info
        Given Credit 300
        Outstanding 100
        Net Position 0
        Bet Credit 200
      • Example 2: Total Payout Away Wins > Total Stake > Total Payout Home Wins
        On bet type Match Winner, member A has placed 2 tickets:
        • Ticket 1 placed on Home team with stake $100 and odds 1.8.
        • Ticket 2 placed on Away team with stake $100 and odds 2.02.
        The possible outcome of these tickets will be:
        Bet Tickets Possible Outcome
        # Bet type Bet team Stake Odds Home Wins Away Wins
        1 Match Winner Home 100 1.8 180 0
        2 Match Winner Away 100 2.02 0 202
        Total 200 180 202
        • Ticket 1 is bet on Home Team
          • If Home team wins => Payout Home Wins = $180
          • If Away teams wins => Payout Away Wins = 0
        • Ticket 2 is bet on Away team
          • If Home team wins => Payout Home Wins = 0
          • If Away teams wins => Payout Away Wins = $202
        • In total
          - Total Payout Home Wins = $180 => Net Position = $180
          - Total Payout Away Wins = $202
          - Total Actual Stake = $200
        • Member Balance Info will be displayed as below:
          Member Balance Info
          Given Credit 300
          Outstanding 200
          Net Position 180
          Bet Credit 280

        Note: The Net Position amount has been added into member’s Bet Credit already.

        Bet Credit = Given Credit – Outstanding + Net Position

      • Example 3: Total Payout Away Wins > Total Payout Home Wins > Total Stake
        On bet type Match Winner, member A has placed 2 tickets:
        • Ticket 1 placed on Home team with stake $100 and odds 2.1.
        • Ticket 2 placed on Away team with stake $100 and odds 2.3.
        The possible outcome of these tickets will be:
        Bet Tickets Possible Outcome
        # Bet type Bet team Stake Odds Home Wins Away Wins
        1 Match Winner Home 100 2.1 210 0
        2 Match Winner Away 100 2.3 0 230
        Total 200 210 230
        • Ticket 1 is bet on Home Team
          • If Home team wins => Payout Home Wins = $210
          • If Away teams wins => Payout Away Wins = 0
        • Ticket 2 is bet on Away team
          • If Home team wins => Payout Home Wins = 0
          • If Away teams wins => Payout Away Wins = $230
        • In total
          - Total Payout Home Wins = $210 => Net Position = $200
          - Total Payout Away Wins = $230
          - Total Actual Stake = $200
        • Member Balance Info will be displayed as below:
          Member Balance Info
          Given Credit 300
          Outstanding 200
          Net Position 200
          Bet Credit 300
